ZeroTrace — OSS Grant Summary
📌 Project Overview
ZeroTrace is an open‑source Chrome extension that automates the discovery and removal of a user’s own social‑media activity across Facebook, Twitter, and (soon) Reddit. It solves a real privacy and digital‑hygiene problem that major platforms do not address.

👤 Maintainer
ZeroTrace is actively maintained by Jim McKinstry, who develops new features, maintains platform compatibility, and manages ongoing updates as social‑media interfaces evolve.

🌐 Open‑Source Impact
ZeroTrace fills a critical gap in the privacy ecosystem by providing a transparent, open‑source alternative to proprietary deletion tools. It empowers users to control their digital footprint and reduces exposure from old posts, comments, and interactions.
